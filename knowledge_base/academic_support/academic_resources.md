# Academic Support and Performance Recovery Strategies

## Overview
Students facing academic difficulties often require structured intervention in course concept mastery, internal assessment preparation, and exam recovery plans.

## 1. Internal Assessment & Coursework Recovery
- **Target Situation**: Low internal marks (< 50%) or poor assignment scores.
- **Action Strategy**:
  - Request 1-on-1 tutoring sessions with course teaching assistants or faculty mentors.
  - Submit draft assignments early for formative feedback before final grading.
  - Form peer study groups focusing on problem-solving workshops for difficult modules.

## 2. Exam Preparation & Revision Framework
- **Target Situation**: Exam score drop or fear of upcoming final exams.
- **Action Strategy**:
  - Practice past examination papers under timed conditions twice weekly.
  - Utilize active recall and spaced repetition flashcards for core theoretical concepts.
  - Focus high-effort study blocks on weightage-heavy syllabus chapters.

## 3. Faculty Consultation & Office Hours
- **Target Situation**: Conceptual confusion or difficulty keeping up with lecture speed.
- **Action Strategy**:
  - Schedule bi-weekly office hours check-ins with subject instructors.
  - Maintain a specific query log noting exact topics requiring clarification during lectures.
