# Student Attendance and Engagement Recovery Guidelines

## Overview
Regular lecture attendance and active classroom participation are strong predictors of student retention and overall academic success.

## 1. Attendance Monitoring & Habit Building
- **Target Situation**: Low attendance rate (< 75%) or chronic absenteeism.
- **Action Strategy**:
  - Establish a non-negotiable morning routine and commute planning to ensure punctual lecture arrival.
  - Set up attendance alerts when course attendance drops near or below institutional thresholds (75%).
  - Arrange peer accountability partners to check in before daily lectures.

## 2. Active Classroom Participation
- **Target Situation**: Low class participation score (< 50%) despite lecture attendance.
- **Action Strategy**:
  - Sit in the front rows to increase focus and instructor interaction.
  - Prepare 1-2 questions prior to class based on pre-lecture reading materials.
  - Contribute actively to group discussions and interactive polling sessions.

## 3. Re-engaging After Missed Classes
- **Target Situation**: Academic backlog due to illness or approved leaves.
- **Action Strategy**:
  - Access recorded lecture archives and official slide decks within 24 hours of missed classes.
  - Obtain lecture notes from designated peer note-takers.
  - Schedule a brief catch-up meeting with class instructors.
