# Time Management, Stress Relief, and Academic Mentoring

## Overview
Comprehensive student support requires balancing academic responsibilities, personal well-being, time management, and structured mentorship.

## 1. Academic Advising & Peer Mentoring
- **Target Situation**: Students at Medium or High risk needing holistic guidance.
- **Action Strategy**:
  - Assign a senior student peer mentor for weekly 30-minute academic progress reviews.
  - Meet monthly with an assigned academic advisor to evaluate degree progression and risk factors.
  - Participate in campus academic success workshops on stress management and exam mindset.

## 2. Priority Management & Time Blocking
- **Target Situation**: Overwhelmed with multiple deadlines, poor previous semester performance.
- **Action Strategy**:
  - Categorize weekly tasks using the Eisenhower Matrix (Urgent vs. Important).
  - Allocate dedicated buffer time on weekends for revision and rest.
  - Eliminate digital distractions during designated deep work study sessions.

## 3. Institutional Support & Counseling Resources
- **Target Situation**: High academic anxiety, burnout, or personal factors impacting studies.
- **Action Strategy**:
  - Connect with student counseling centers for confidential stress and anxiety management.
  - Access writing centers and math learning assistance labs for foundational skill building.
