# Effective Study Planning and Self-Regulated Learning

## Overview
Optimizing weekly study hours and active study techniques significantly boosts academic retention and exam outcomes.

## 1. Weekly Study Schedule Optimization
- **Target Situation**: Low weekly study hours (< 10 hours/week) or unstructured study habits.
- **Action Strategy**:
  - Implement a fixed 15-20 hour weekly dedicated study routine divided into 90-minute focused blocks.
  - Apply the Pomodoro Technique (25 min intense study, 5 min break) to sustain focus and prevent burnout.
  - Track study hours using a digital log or study tracking app.

## 2. Active Learning & Note-Taking Techniques
- **Target Situation**: Ineffective passive reading, high study hours with low grade return.
- **Action Strategy**:
  - Use the Cornell Note-Taking method during lectures and textbook reviews.
  - Summarize key concepts in own words rather than re-reading highlighting notes.
  - Teach concepts aloud to peers or explain complex theories in simple summaries (Feynman Technique).

## 3. Assignment Completion Workflow
- **Target Situation**: Late assignment submission or low assignment average (< 60%).
- **Action Strategy**:
  - Break large project deliverables into micro-tasks scheduled across 5-7 days before deadline.
  - Set personal artificial deadlines 48 hours prior to official course deadlines.
